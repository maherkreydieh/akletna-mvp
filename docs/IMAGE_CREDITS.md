## Nutrition data

Ingredient nutrition values come from USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy (April 2018), a public-domain US government dataset. Three ingredients use approximations because SR Legacy has no exact entry: pomegranate molasses (molasses), sumac (paprika), and "Garlic & Dried Mint" (garlic).
